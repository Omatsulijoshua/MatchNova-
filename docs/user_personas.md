# User Personas - MatchNova

To design an intuitive and user-centered dating platform, we establish four primary personas representing diverse segments of our target audience.

---

## Persona 1: Sarah, 29 - The High-Achieving Professional
* **Demographics**: Senior Software Engineer in San Francisco, CA. Single, No kids.
* **Tech Stack Preference**: iOS Mobile App, Safari Web.
* **Goals**: Finds it hard to meet compatible partners due to long working hours. Wants a long-term, stable relationship with a peer who shares similar career aspirations and values.
* **Pain Points with Competitors**:
  * Tinder/Bumble: Low-effort bios, endless small talk that fizzles out, superficial matching.
  * The League: Elite-focused but feels overly pretentious and slow to match.
* **How MatchNova Solves It**:
  * AI-powered semantic matching screens out low-effort or incompatible profiles automatically.
  * AI-suggested icebreakers jumpstart conversations with meaningful prompts based on shared tech/industry interests.
  * Strict education and career verification badges ensure profile credibility.

---

## Persona 2: Marcus, 24 - The Social Casual Dater
* **Demographics**: Graphic Designer in New York, NY. Single, No kids.
* **Tech Stack Preference**: Android Mobile App, Chrome Web.
* **Goals**: Wants to date casually, explore the city, meet new people, and find common creative interests.
* **Pain Points with Competitors**:
  * OkCupid: Questionnaire takes too long; app feels bloated.
  * Happn: Only matches near immediate paths, which limits variety.
* **How MatchNova Solves It**:
  * Conversational onboarding uses natural language inputs instead of long, tedious questionnaires to capture preferences.
  * Fuzzy Geofencing matches him with people who frequent similar creative hubs (parks, design districts) without compromising safety.
  * The visual card stack UI is fast, responsive, and aesthetically engaging.

---

## Persona 3: Elena, 33 - The Security & Privacy Advocate
* **Demographics**: Medical Practitioner in Chicago, IL. Single, No kids.
* **Tech Stack Preference**: iOS Mobile App.
* **Goals**: Wants a safe environment to explore relationships. Needs complete control over who can view her profile and when her location is active.
* **Pain Points with Competitors**:
  * Tinder: Unsolicited explicit images, fake profiles, catfishing, stalker risks.
  * Badoo: Low profile verification standard.
* **How MatchNova Solves It**:
  * Real-time Media Moderation screens out NSFW images before they are loaded on her screen.
  * Strict selfie face-matching verification processes during registration.
  * Advanced privacy options that completely mask her work and home coordinates, while still calculating overall match compatibility.

---

## Persona 4: David, 42 - The Mature Relationship Seeker
* **Demographics**: Financial Consultant in Toronto, ON. Divorced, Father of one.
* **Tech Stack Preference**: Web Application (Next.js Desktop), iOS Mobile App.
* **Goals**: Seeking a second marriage/long-term partnership. Needs clear filters for family goals, children, and lifestyle.
* **Pain Points with Competitors**:
  * eHarmony: Outdated, slow-moving UI, extremely expensive monthly subscription.
  * Tinder: Demographic is too young, features are too game-like.
* **How MatchNova Solves It**:
  * Clean, accessible UI with adjustable font sizes and desktop compatibility.
  * Multi-dimensional filtering that prioritizes long-term compatibility, parenting styles, and life goals.
  * A transparent subscription model integrated via Stripe for clean invoicing and secure payments.
